# Creative judgment for a specific brief

Use for a new visual direction, a substantial `identity`/`compose` request, or a complaint that designs feel interchangeable. A small correction does not need a new concept study. This is design-pro's synthesis; book evidence is in [creative reading notes](creative-sources.md), separate from these operating instructions.

The aim is to make perceptive, deliberate design choices that survive the brief and rendered use. Loading books supplies context and examples; it does not retrain model weights or establish artistic mastery. Show learning through changed decisions, comparisons and revisions.

## Keep the brief present

Extract the audience, purpose, essential content, primary action, existing identity, platform and protected behavior. Separate those invariants from choices still open to exploration. A playful composition may change the treatment of a release date, but cannot remove it or invent a trailer. An app's personality cannot obscure sending, progress or recovery. Reuse a sufficient saved brief; do not interview the user again merely to start creative work.

The user delegates creative judgment. Research, develop and recommend a direction rather than requiring them to choose a style name or approve every aesthetic choice. Ask only about genuinely consequential missing intent. Preserve existing authorization and the isolated-alternative boundary of `compare`.

## Study relationships, then transform them

Select the relevant lens in the reading notes. Inspect actual interior pages/figures for claims about visual form, not only a cover, abstract or someone else's summary. For new book research under this user's preference, verify a public GitHub PDF: record the exact file/revision, download it, check its header/readability, edition/language and relevant pages. HTML, a source-code repository and an external PDF link alone do not meet that condition. Do not claim full reading from selected passages.

Describe what the work is doing in concrete terms: a dominant mass against a small counterweight; a recurring edge; dense versus quiet regions; a color used over a large area versus a small accent; the relationship between image, letterform and task. Explain the visible relationship before naming a style. Keep observation, author claim and your inference distinct.

Borrow a relationship or method and transform it using this project's subject. Do not transplant a book's logo, artwork, house style or complete layout. Look beyond UI galleries when appropriate: subject matter, physical objects, editorial composition, signage, craft and natural structures can supply material. Do not invent having inspected a reference.

## Develop a visual idea before a component recipe

Use the content's nouns, verbs, tensions or transformations to find an idea. For a garden restoration game, orbit/growth, broken/connected or absence/return offer different starting points; 'modern premium landing page' offers little direction. Roughly externalize the plausible ideas through a thumbnail, typographic arrangement or small browser study before polishing one. The useful number depends on uncertainty, not a mandatory three.

A meaningful alternative changes how the subject is represented, how attention moves or how information relates. Recoloring the same hero and card grid is not enough. Judge the complete composition, not a pile of impressive components. The user prefers open grouping; this does not prescribe beige, muted green, serif headings, huge margins or a gentle personality for every project.

## Exercise judgment and commit within scope

Choose a direction by its specific connection to the brief, visual coherence, distinctiveness and usability. Brief violations are failures, not points that a beautiful illustration can compensate for. Explain a rejected idea when its tradeoff informs the decision; do not dump every rough sketch on the user. Creativity may live in composition, image making, type, language or interaction. It need not appear in all of them.

Translate the chosen idea into a connected visual grammar: scale/spacing rhythm, type roles, color roles/area, imagery and the few component details that actually benefit. Keep routine operations familiar when novelty would burden the task. Web can sustain a strongly expressive opening with legible facts/actions; Mobile must also work through repeated reading, keyboard/input, history and interrupted states. A native app retains its platform behavior.

## Critique the rendered work and revise

Inspect the actual wide/narrow or relevant mobile states. Describe a visible weakness and make a concrete revision: an illustration competes with the action; every line has equal weight; the intended motif disappears when the view narrows; decorative geometry distorts the label. Recheck the affected behavior after revision.

As a diagnostic, imagine swapping the name and content with an unrelated product. If almost everything still fits, find the missing connection; this is a judgment prompt, not a scientific originality test. Conversely, conspicuous novelty with weak relevance is not a reason to keep it. Compare against the existing product and previous project decisions without assuming all familiar patterns are wrong.

Report concise evidence: what the concept communicates, a consequential choice/rejection, an observed revision, brief conformance, and what remains untested. Screenshots support composition; interaction checks support behavior. Neither proves audience preference, improved conversion or general model improvement. Save authorized project decisions and practice notes with the project. Global skill changes require a user request; do not silently accumulate universal aesthetic rules from each task.

## Select the needed method

Read the relevant section of [creative design](concepts/creative-design.md) when a task needs more than this workflow: concept development for `identity`; composition/visual tension for `compose`; expressive component shape for `craft`; critique/selection for `diagnose`, `compare` or `prove`. `resolve` selects only what its diagnosis calls for. The concept reference contains concrete DOs, DON'Ts, decision guidelines and citations; it is also the destination for creative concept queries. A query remains explanatory and does not create designs or edit files.
