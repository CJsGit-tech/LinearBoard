# Credibility, reassurance and truthful presentation

Credibility comes from accurate information and behavior that keeps its promise. Use visual order to aid comprehension, and back claims of security, reliability or customers with the real thing. [X09, p. 23](../books/X09.md#ux-usefulness-and-evidence); [X10, pp. 87–105](../books/X10.md#credibility-and-truthful-evidence)

## DO

- Label the action and deliver the expected result; keep pending, failed and completed operations distinct. [X10, pp. 25–26, 93–94](../books/X10.md)
- State actual availability, costs, delivery expectations and material conditions where they matter. [X10, pp. 102–104](../books/X10.md)
- Use real, correctly attributed testimonials, ratings and results, with definitions, dates and limits where numbers need context. [X10, pp. 95–101 and qualifications](../books/X10.md#credibility-and-truthful-evidence)
- Label simulated services and fictional content in demos, and keep design rationale and citations in companion documents *(synthesis)*.

## DON'T

- Do not fabricate reviews, users, endorsements, security badges or release availability.
- Do not use a lock icon, blue palette or rounded form as a stand-in for actual protection. [X08, p. 9](../books/X08.md#communicate-the-offering-and-the-operation); [X10, pp. 89–90, 103](../books/X10.md)
- Do not show fake Save/Refresh actions or artificial waiting *(synthesis; rejects [X07, pp. 11–17](../books/X07.md#deliberate-rejection-of-deceptive-controls))*.
- Do not conceal material terms or obstruct cancellation to lift a conversion number. [X10, pp. 93–94](../books/X10.md)

## Guidelines and example

Treat perceived credibility and verified capability as separate questions. With no genuine social proof, improve clarity and show the actual product. [X10, pp. 88–101](../books/X10.md)

For a fictional game, use original sample release details and gameplay descriptions, label the page a concept demo, and make the signup confirmation say it is a local simulation. For a chatbot, retry acts on a real failed state of the simulated response *(synthesis)*.

## Appearance evidence has a limited scope

The often-cited Stanford figure counts coded comments that mention design look; cite it only as that. [U17, p. 10 and focused primary-report cross-check, verified 2026-10-25](../books/U17.md#credibility-claim-checked-against-its-primary-source)

Assess perceived clarity separately from task completion, accuracy and reliability. Choose rounded rectangles, logo position, color mood or a human photo for the context. [U17, pp. 10–21, 27–38](../books/U17.md)
