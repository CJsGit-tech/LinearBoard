# Notifications and interruption

A notification should help a recipient act on a relevant event. Determine the benefit, urgency and channel before designing the prompt. A badge inside an app, an in-app status message and an OS/browser push have different purposes and permission implications. [M07, pp. 7, 10–12](../books/M07.md#principles-worth-retaining); [M06, pp. 35–37](../books/M06.md#input-and-action)

## DO

- Explain the benefit when the user has enough context to choose. Deliver only the kind of message promised. [M07, pp. 18–19](../books/M07.md#principles-worth-retaining)
- Make the action clear and take the recipient to the relevant destination. [M07, pp. 12, 21–22](../books/M07.md)
- Consider frequency, timezone, urgency and recipient preferences. Watch fatigue and opt-outs alongside task outcomes. [M07, pp. 22, 24](../books/M07.md)
- For an in-app update, make new activity understandable without relying solely on a colored dot. This is design-pro synthesis from [M06, pp. 35–37](../books/M06.md) and its accessibility checks.

## DON'T

- Do not nag after refusal or disguise a promotional message as a necessary service alert.
- Do not invent urgency or assume location/history data are authorized merely because personalization is possible.
- Do not infer a causal retention improvement from opted-in versus opted-out cohorts.
- Do not promise that delivery means a person saw or understood the message.

These qualifications and the historical source's reach-calculation problems are explained in [M07](../books/M07.md#evidence-limitations).

## Guidelines and example

The source's 60–120-character suggestion is a heuristic, not an OS limit. Check truncation with the actual language and presentation. Current native/browser permissions and support need primary-source verification for the chosen platform. The old browser matrix is not a compatibility reference. [M07, pp. 11, 19, 21](../books/M07.md#boundaries-and-disagreements)

A game page may offer an optional release reminder after the visitor chooses to follow the game. A local demo should state that the reminder signup is simulated and avoid requesting real push permission solely for demonstration. This is design-pro application.
