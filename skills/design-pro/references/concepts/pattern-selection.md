# Pattern selection, consistency and signifiers

A pattern is a candidate solution to a recurring problem. Choose it by task, context and observed behavior; popularity and visual resemblance do not establish fitness. [X05, pp. 13–21, 45–48](../books/X05.md#patterns-are-hypotheses-about-familiar-problems)

## DO

- State the problem before browsing examples, and compare the trigger, visibility, representation and result of candidate patterns. [X05, pp. 45–48](../books/X05.md#compare-the-mechanism-not-merely-the-screenshot)
- Make the perceived action match the capability: a favorite, save or refresh control should perform what it implies. [X05, pp. 32–33](../books/X05.md#capability-signifier-and-expectation); the rejection of fake controls is design-pro judgment in [X07](../books/X07.md#deliberate-rejection-of-deceptive-controls).
- Keep meaning and repeated behavior consistent while allowing deliberate variants for different contexts. Document those conditions. [X05, pp. 38–44](../books/X05.md#choose-at-several-levels); [S02, pp. 27–41](../books/S02.md#practical-guidance)
- If departing from convention, explain the intended improvement and test recognition and task completion. [X05, pp. 24–31](../books/X05.md#evaluate-change-rather-than-copy-convention)

## DON'T

- Do not assume a familiar icon needs no label or accessible name for the intended audience.
- Do not copy hover-only or gesture-only access to an essential action.
- Do not impose fixed card dimensions or one component variant everywhere under the name of consistency.
- Do not claim a pattern guarantees dopamine, trust, happiness or conversion. The source does not establish such causal guarantees. [X05, pp. 8, 22–27](../books/X05.md#unsupported-mechanisms-and-dated-resources)

## Guidelines and example

Resolve the structural need and main flow before polishing isolated controls. The source's context/strategy/consistency categories are useful thinking tools, not rigid phases or universal feature requirements. Test the actual representation with the relevant input methods. [X05, pp. 38–48](../books/X05.md)

For a mobile chatbot, use the existing conversation-list pattern where available and keep sending/retrying behavior coherent. A public game page may use in-page links rather than that app navigation. Both can share tokens while serving different tasks; this is design-pro application.

## External and internal consistency

External consistency draws on the audience's prior conventions; internal consistency keeps a product's own cues, labels and behavior predictable. Inspect both before changing a pattern. Do not presume everyone knows an icon or reverse an established required-field symbol without clear explanation. [U03, pp. 12–23](../books/U03.md)

Preserve intentional variants for different roles/states: a primary action and a destructive action need not look identical. A useful exception can improve a task, such as avoiding a logo that resembles a menu control. Document the reason and test the affected behavior. Do not make cosmetic changes merely to avoid supposed boredom, or obstruct leaving a flow in the name of focus. [U03, pp. 24–37](../books/U03.md#purposeful-differences-and-historical-cases)

## Minimalism and necessary complexity

Simplify by removing elements that do not help the task while retaining understandable actions, necessary content and recovery. A restrained aesthetic can use color, imagery or asymmetry; it does not require an empty white screen, a hamburger menu or one action everywhere. Alignment is not the same as centering. [U13, pp. 6–18, 22–24, 32–33](../books/U13.md)

Do not impose five-section/top-five quotas, hide important product details behind hover, make secondary body copy unreadable, or assume children/gamers dislike simple interfaces. Assess the actual content and audience. A sparse visual surface also does not prove fast loading: verify the delivered implementation. [U13, pp. 19–28](../books/U13.md#discoverability-and-readability-survive-subtraction)

For a chatbot, fewer nested surfaces can improve grouping, but removing retry or conversation navigation would remove necessary function. For a game page, expressive artwork may serve identity while a short readable release block supports the decision. These are design-pro examples, not a mandate to restyle an existing product.
