# Cards and grouping

A card presents one coherent content unit or decision. Use it for independent summaries, discovery or context-preserving detail. [M03, pp. 8–10, 16–22](../books/M03.md)

## DO

[M03, pp. 8–10, 19, 21–27](../books/M03.md#principles-and-applications)

- Decide what the unit means and its primary interaction before drawing its surface.
- Use proximity to associate an author, message and metadata.
- Separate compact summaries from expanded detail when the task needs both.
- Keep content readable and activation regions usable as the layout reflows.

## DON'T

- Do not wrap every paragraph or list row in nested borders; group by proximity.
- Do not rely on a container to make a gesture discoverable; add a signifier.
- Do not disguise advertising as ordinary content or make dismissal hard to activate *(synthesis; rejects [M03, pp. 12–13](../books/M03.md#exceptions-and-source-critique))*.

## Guidelines and example

For comparing the same attributes across many records, use a table or aligned list. For choosing among independent visual stories, use cards *(synthesis)*.

In chat, group each turn without an outer card around every avatar, bubble and timestamp. On a release page, use a container only for an independently meaningful item.

## Organization and adaptation

A card can hold several related fields about one subject. Set its hierarchy and density by the task. Build reflow and usable controls into the implementation. [U12, pp. 6–20, 26–27](../books/U12.md)

Card sorting is a research method for grouping and labels; it says nothing about using card-shaped UI. [U12, p. 13](../books/U12.md#organization-and-responsive-behavior-require-actual-decisions)

Size images by their value to the unit; a text-only card is fine. Give required actions a route without hover. Keep reading and focus order meaningful when a grid changes. Include secondary controls, imagery and sharing only when useful. [U12, pp. 19–24](../books/U12.md#retained-practices-and-limits)

## Validate the content unit and its states

Assess a grid with realistic title lengths, metadata and state variations. Check targets, responsive behavior and overlay focus in the implementation. [W03, PDF pp. 45–72](../books/W03.md#minimal-appearance-and-cards-are-choices)

When a whole card links somewhere, keep secondary controls, text selection and keyboard navigation working, with no nested conflicting actions. [W07, PDF pp. 13–14](../books/W07.md#ten-candidate-techniques-and-their-limits)
