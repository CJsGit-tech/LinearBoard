# unframe — 解除多餘視覺分割

## Responsibility

Which boundaries are fragmenting the page without helping understanding?

Read the [shared execution contract](../execution.md) and the applicable mode.

## Execute

Inventory enclosing borders, cards, pills, divider bars, icon circles, nested fills and decorative shadows. Classify each by the relationship, action or state it communicates. Remove redundant framing and replace the lost grouping with proximity, alignment and type hierarchy where necessary. Preserve boundaries that are needed for a real layer, data relationship or functional state. Inspect the entire composition again; merely fading all border colors does not resolve structural fragmentation.

## Platform focus

- **Web:** integrate navigation, metadata and open content while retaining meaningful tables, dialogs and selected states.
- **Mobile:** simplify repeated prompt/utility containers without shrinking targets or making entry/send difficult to discover.

## Deliver and finish

an open composition plus a short account of the boundaries removed and the reasons for those retained.

Completion: redundant frames are removed, grouping remains legible, and the affected controls/states still work. Zero borders is not the success criterion.

## Example

```text
$design-pro unframe mobile "Chat homepage: keep grouping but remove the fragmented-card feeling"
```
