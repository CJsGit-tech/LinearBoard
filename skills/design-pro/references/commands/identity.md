# identity — 建立產品辨識度

## Responsibility

What makes this interface recognizably appropriate to this product?

Read the [shared execution contract](../execution.md) and the applicable mode.

## Execute

For a new direction or an interchangeable result, use [creative judgment](../creative-judgment.md). Keep the brief's invariants visible while exploring; make and justify a creative recommendation when that judgment is delegated to you.

Extract the product's purpose, audience, content, established assets and desired character from confirmed context. Translate them into a small connected set of expressive choices: typography, semantic color, imagery/icon treatment, content tone and motion character where useful. Avoid a generic decorative recipe. Make those choices work with real content and existing platform conventions. If the direction is unresolved and consequential, compare explicit tradeoffs rather than silently rebranding. Keep the user's open-composition preference and the task intact.

## Creative application

When developing character, use [concept development](../concepts/creative-design.md#concept-development) and [motifs/coherence](../concepts/creative-design.md#motifs-and-coherence). Begin with the brief's distinctive nouns, verbs and relationships; turn promising associations into rough visual arrangements before selecting a UI kit. Translate the chosen idea into connected image/type/spacing/component decisions. A leaf icon plus green buttons is not enough to explain what makes this particular garden product different.

Recommend the direction and explain its decisive tradeoff when judgment is delegated. Do not require the user to choose a style label, treat a favorite book's look as a house style, or silently replace a confirmed identity. Show the relationship between the concept and a visible choice, keeping sources in companion rationale. In Mobile, carry character into repeated reading and states without making every turn decorative.

## Platform focus

- **Web:** relate release/marketing expression to product facts and the intended next action.
- **Mobile:** express character within repeated reading, input and platform behavior without sacrificing familiarity.

## Deliver and finish

a justified visual direction and scoped design application, or clearly labeled alternatives when direction remains open.

Completion: the expression is tied to the product, remains coherent across the target's states and does not obscure the task. Do not claim universal audience emotions or measured brand recognition.

## Example

```text
$design-pro identity web "ORBITAL: quiet exploration and regrowth, preserving the existing game facts"
```
