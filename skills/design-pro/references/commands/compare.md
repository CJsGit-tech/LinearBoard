# compare — 比較不同設計假設

## Responsibility

Which materially different design approach best satisfies this task?

Read the [shared execution contract](../execution.md) and the applicable mode.

## Execute

For creative directions, use [creative judgment](../creative-judgment.md) to compare representation, attention and content relationships. Show why the recommended direction is specifically suited to the brief; variety alone does not establish quality.

Frame the unresolved design decision and hold product facts, essential functionality and comparison conditions constant. Produce two or three candidates that test distinct hypotheses, such as open grouping versus progressive disclosure. Do not call recolored copies different design directions. Evaluate tradeoffs against the agreed criteria, identify what would contradict the recommendation, and show comparable artifacts at the same relevant size/state. Recommend a candidate with evidence; user preference and actual user research remain separate. Keep alternatives isolated until selection or explicit adoption authority exists.

## Creative application

Use [critique and selection](../concepts/creative-design.md#critique-and-selection) with the relevant concept/composition lens. Compare how each candidate represents the subject and organizes attention, not merely its palette or component skin. Keep required content, primary action and relevant state/viewport conditions equivalent. Candidates may retain the same brand colors while testing different visual ideas.

Describe each candidate's specific strength and cost; reject or repair a brief violation rather than averaging it into an aesthetic score. Recommend one direction with a reason grounded in the visible work, and identify what remains an audience hypothesis. Explain a useful rejected idea without making the user sift through every sketch. Preserve the isolated-candidate adoption boundary.

## Platform focus

- **Web:** compare the same task at relevant wide/narrow conditions.
- **Mobile:** include realistic entry, keyboard/available-space and return behavior when material, not only a static welcome screen.

## Deliver and finish

distinct comparable candidates, tradeoffs and a recommendation. No automatic replacement of the main source.

Completion: the candidates clarify the actual decision and their comparison uses equivalent content/conditions; no fabricated test result or conversion estimate is included.

## Example

```text
$design-pro compare web "Two ways to present dense order details while preserving record comparison"
```
