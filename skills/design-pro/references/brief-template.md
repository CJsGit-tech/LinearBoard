# Project brief

Use the fields necessary for this request; omit irrelevant empty sections.

- **Mode and platform:** Mobile / Web Apps / both; native versus mobile web when consequential.
- **Requested outcome and deliverable:** what changes, and what artifact is authorized.
- **Users and context:** known needs, setting, interruptions and accessibility considerations.
- **Primary task and success:** trigger, action, outcome and observable success criteria.
- **Scope:** essential journeys and exclusions.
- **Existing product:** applicable instructions, reusable components, tokens and established interactions.
- **Visual direction and content:** supplied references, brand attributes and available assets.
- **Constraints:** technical, platform, content and service limitations.
- **Confirmed requirements:** direct user statements or inspected project facts, with origin.
- **Assumptions:** reasonable provisional choices and their implications.
- **Unresolved decisions:** only decisions still material to the work.
- **Current decisions and verification:** choices made, rationale and evidence still needed.
- **Design criteria when useful:** connect necessary flows/states to the intended outcome and observable acceptance conditions; distinguish local checks from user evidence.

For a fictional demonstration, label the fictional scenario and sample content explicitly. Do not attribute demonstration choices to the user.
